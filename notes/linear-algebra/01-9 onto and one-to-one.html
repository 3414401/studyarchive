Onto & One-to-One

Onto(전사)와 단사(One-to-one)

Onto 전사

도달 가능한가?(Existence): 공역의 모든 점에 도달할 수 있는가?

A mapping T: ℝn -> ℝm is said to be onto if each b in ℝm is the image of at least one x in ℝn.

✔️검사법: A의 모든 행에 pivot위치가 존재한다.

One-to-One 단사

유일한가?(Uniqueness): 각 결과값에 도달하는 경로는 단 하나뿐인가?

A mapping T: ℝn -> ℝm is said to be one-to-one if each b in ℝm is the image of at most one x in ℝn.

✔️검사법: free variable 이 존재하지 않는다. [A 0]말고 그냥 [A]! 그냥 [A]의 열들이 선형독립인지 봐야함.

🔖Theorem 4

Let A be an m×n matrix. Then the following statements are logically equivalent. That is, for a particular A, either they are all true statements or they are all false.

a. For each b in ℝm, the equation Ax=b has a solution.

b. Each b in ℝm is a linear combination of the columns of A.

c. The columns of A span ℝm.

⭐d. A has a pivot position in every row.

🔖Theorem 11

Let T: ℝn -> ℝm be a linear transformation. Then T is one-to-one if and only if the equation T(x)=0 has only the trivial solution.

🔖Theorem 12

Let T: ℝn -> ℝm be a linear transformation, and let A be the standard matrix for T. Then:

a. T maps ℝn onto ℝm if and only the columns of A span ℝm.

b. T is one-to-oneif and only if ⭐the columns of A are linearly independent.⭐
